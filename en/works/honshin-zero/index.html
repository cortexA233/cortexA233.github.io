Honshin Zero

2025 · unity · action · 3d · prototype · 1 min

itch.io

A responsive 3D combat prototype made for a recruitment test. Our two-person team built it in about five days; I handled programming. Playable in the browser.

Combat & controls

  • WASD / left click — movement and five-hit combos.
  • Shift / right click — dodge with invincibility.
  • E — charge a counter; striking an attacking enemy extends the combo.
  • Q — an airborne ultimate that deals sustained area damage.
  • Esc — toggle the cursor.

Some character models and animations were supplied by miHoYo and are used for non-commercial presentation. Third-party assets belong to their respective owners.
