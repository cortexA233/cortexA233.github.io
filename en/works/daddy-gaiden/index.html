Daddy Gaiden

2025 · unity · action · rogue-like · 1 min

itch.io

A 2D action roguelike prototype inspired by Ninja Gaiden 2 and Hades, aiming to bring that "high-speed action" combat feel into a 2D side-scrolling framework, with rogue-like elements for replayability. Built during a game jam; I was the lead designer, combat designer and lead programmer.

  • Designed the full combat system and a matching resource-management system to make each run feel punchy and rewarding.
  • Implemented all of the game's functionality, with extra polish on the combat.
  • Self-managed the project: set the task schedule, coordinated contributors, and shipped within the game-jam window.

Playing the demo

Submitted to Beginners' Jam Winter 2025/6. Listed as in development on itch.io, with browser, Windows and macOS versions; the project page recommends downloading.

Slash fruits and survive fast-paced combat: WASD to move, left click / J to attack, right click / K for Boost Mode, Shift to block, and Space to dodge.
