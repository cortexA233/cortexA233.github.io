Golden Retriever

2023 · unity · action · 2d · game-jam · 1 min

Play on itch.io

You are a clever, nimble golden retriever who finds a sword and shield in the forest—and figures out how to wield both.

Project snapshot

  • Origin — BOOOM 2023 (May).
  • Team — five credited creators.
  • Platforms — HTML5 and Windows.
  • Technology — Unity, powered by KToolkit.

Core mechanic

  • Left mouse button — launch the shield; hold to charge a longer throw.
  • Right mouse button — recall the shield.
  • Space — drop the sword; press again to return to it.
  • While returning, you first fly to any shield still in the field and then to the sword. You can launch the shield again along the way.
