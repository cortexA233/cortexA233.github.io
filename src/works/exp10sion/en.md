A puzzle platformer built around a **"10-second loop"** rule. The Ludum Dare 51 prototype grew
into the first chapter of a full, commercially released title (Steam + Nintendo
Switch). Team of 3–6; I served as **lead programmer, project manager and level designer**.

Reach the goal within ten seconds before exploding, and solve puzzles with another
"you". itch.io hosts browser and Windows demos; the full game is on Steam and Nintendo Switch.

The Nintendo Switch edition is listed as **10-Second Ghost**.

## What I did

- **Design** — proposed the core concept and the "10-second loop" rule, and built the game-jam prototype that became the game's first chapter; tuned the platforming feel referencing industry cases and GDC talks.
- **Programming** — built the game's logic framework **KToolkit** (reused in later projects): a name-based global event system, a config-driven page / page-stack system with lifecycle management, and an extensible `MonoBehaviour`-bound finite-state machine (FSM).
- **Project management** — set the schedule and milestones, organized art assets and versioning with the design lead, ran weekly meetings and coordinated owners.
- **Launch** — integrated the Steam SDK (stats, achievements, leaderboards), reached out to curators, streamers and indie KOLs, and iterated on player feedback from expos and online communities.
