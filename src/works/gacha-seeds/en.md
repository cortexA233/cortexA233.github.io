A first-person ecological gardening prototype for **Climate Jam / IndieCade 2026**. Tend a greenhouse laboratory in a polluted near-future world.

## Gameplay

Draw seed capsules, plant and water them, harvest beneficial plants, and clear harmful weeds with a UV-C purifier. Collect 100 plant samples before the smog countdown expires to complete an anti-pollution serum.
