A 2D roguelite shooter originally made for **BOOOM 2022**, in which you fire your
own bodies.

## Project snapshot

- **Origin** — BOOOM 2022 game jam.
- **Status** — itch.io lists it as in development and describes a remake in progress.
- **Platforms** — HTML5 and Windows.
- **Technology** — Unity, powered by
  [KToolkit](https://github.com/cortexA233/KToolkit_for_unity).

## Core controls

- **W / A / S / D** — change direction.
- **Left mouse button** — shoot your own bodies.
