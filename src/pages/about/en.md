**Mengxiang Wang (cortexA233)**<br>
**Gameplay / Systems Programmer**

I build gameplay systems, cameras and editor tools in C++ and C#. At **NetEase Games**, I worked on the camera platform, combat and movement replication, and designer tools for *Sea of Remnants*. At **Papergames**, I developed spatial-query and photo-mode systems for *Shining Nikki*.

For the indie game **Exp10sion**, I served as lead programmer, project manager and level designer, helping bring the game to Steam and Nintendo Switch. I also built [KToolkit](https://github.com/cortexA233/KToolkit_for_unity), a Unity game-logic framework reused in later projects, and continue to explore combat feel and gameplay mechanics through small prototypes.

I'm currently pursuing an M.S. in Game Science and Design at **Northeastern University** in Boston (2025–2027), after a B.Eng. in Electronic Engineering from **Shanghai University**.

Since July 2025, I've also been a Software Development Engineer Intern at **Philo Labs**, building agent-evaluation and automated-testing tools for game engines and 3D workflows. This work has expanded my experience with AI-assisted development, cross-engine acceptance tests and reproducible regression testing.

**Tech** — C++, C#, Python, Lua · Unity, Unreal Engine, Godot, Messiah · Qt/PyQt, IMGUI, Git. I use Codex, Claude Code and MCP to support development and testing.

**Languages** — Chinese (native), English (TOEFL iBT 102), Japanese (JLPT N1).
