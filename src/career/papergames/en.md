At **Infold Games (Papergames)**, I developed player-facing features, photo-mode tools, and spatial-query systems for the live Unity title **Shining Nikki**.

**Role:** Game Client Engineer<br>
**Location:** Shanghai, China<br>
**Period:** December 2020 – June 2023<br>
**Technologies:** C#, Lua, Unity

## Minigames and player-facing features

- Independently developed and shipped **2D/3D minigames** for events and the main story, adding interactive gameplay to narrative content.

## Photo-mode tools and 3D synchronization

- Built photo-mode features for stickers with custom text, effects, and transform editing.
- Designed a **dual-anchor positioning algorithm** that synchronized UGUI effects with a 3D character's translation, rotation, and scale.

## Spatial queries and performance

- Optimized **AABB ray tests** and implemented transformed **OBBs**, OBB/AABB intersections, and ray tests in Lua.
- Designed a hybrid **octree/BVH spatial index** for frequent global object queries, making ray queries **2–5× faster than Unity's `RaycastAll`**.
