At **NetEase Games, Joker Studio**, I owned the game-wide camera system and developed ground-combat features and designer tools for **Sea of Remnants**.

**Role:** Senior Game Development Engineer<br>
**Location:** Hangzhou, China<br>
**Period:** November 2023 – May 2025<br>
**Technologies:** C++, Python, Messiah (in-house engine), PyQt, IMGUI

## Game-wide camera system

- Owned the design, implementation, and maintenance of the camera system, standardizing APIs across pipelines and node graphs.
- Implemented **camera inertia, collision handling, and debug commands**; supported other teams in integrating the system and creating camera effects.
- Built reusable, designer-facing **modifiers for position, rotation, viewing distance, and FOV**, including track-based camera movement and dolly zooms.

## Ground combat and skill scripting

- Developed client/server ground-combat features: **movement replication and character positioning** on the server, and **scene loading, character poses, and camera presentation** on the client.
- Extended skill-editor keyframes and event nodes, and exposed **frame events and designer-facing scripting APIs** for authoring complex skills, flow logic, and combat presentation.

## Tools for design and art teams

- Built a **PyQt timeline editor** to author and preview skills, buffs, and cinematics outside combat, reproducing turn-based combat presentation rules.
- Built an **IMGUI runtime character-lighting tool** to support game-specific lighting configuration.
